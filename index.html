The craft

Three days. One blade.

Each Hagane starts as a billet of VG-10 laminated between softer stainless. It's forged, annealed, ground, and hardened to 61 HRC — then a single sharpener spends half a day bringing the edge to 15 degrees per side by hand.

We leave the kurouchi — the dark forge skin — on the upper blade. It's not decoration. It protects the steel and tells you exactly how the knife was made: under a hammer, not a press.
